&6&lBetterBarrels&r &7- store 2,048 of one item in a barrel
&7No menu or recipe: use a placed barrel with your main hand.

&6What it does
&7Each barrel stores up to &f2,048&7 of one exact item type.
&7Names, lore, enchantments and item data must match.
&7The stored count is saved in the barrel's block data.

&6How to use it
&e1. &7Right-click a barrel holding an item to store &fone&7.
&e2. &7Sneak-right-click to store your held stack.
&e3. &7With an empty hand, right-click to take one item.
&e4. &7Sneak-right-click empty-handed for up to one stack.
&7Stack size follows the item: 64, 16, or even 1.
&7Empty the barrel fully to store a different item type.
&7The action bar shows the item and stored count.

&6Recipes
&7There are no crafting recipes. Use a vanilla barrel.

&6Mechanics and limits
&7Capacity is &f2,048&7 items per barrel, any stack size.
&7Barrels have no inventory menu; hoppers cannot move items.
&7Nonempty barrels cannot be broken. Empty barrels can.
&7Fire and explosions cannot destroy barrel storage.
&7Locked barrels need an administrator to unlock them.
&7Spectators cannot use barrel storage.
&7Protection plugins can deny the normal right-click event.
&7Existing barrels with vanilla contents convert to chests.
&7The scan only visits generated chunks; it does not generate terrain.
&7Only currently loaded Bukkit worlds are in scan scope.

&6Commands
&7Players have no storage commands; use right-click controls.
&7Use &f/help betterbarrels&7 for this guide and links.

&7Back up worlds before installation or uninstall conversion.

&7Collect overflow drops, then stop Paper before removing the JAR.
&7Do not remove the plugin if status says RUNNING or PAUSED.

&6Tips and safety
&7Keep backups of worlds and conversion-backups plugin data.
&7A full 64-stack item barrel overflows a chest by 320 items.
&7Overflow is ordinary dropped loot and can despawn.
&7Removing the JAR without conversion hides stored item data.
&7Use every relevant world before a final uninstall scan.
