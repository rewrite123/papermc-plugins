&6&lPhantom Menace&r &7- craft repellant to guard a helmet

&6What it does
&7Phantom Menace I prevents natural phantom spawns
&7that are associated with the player wearing it.
&7It does not reset insomnia or stop phantom attacks.

&6How to use it
&e1. &fBrew Phantom Repellant&7 with water bottles,
&7phantom membranes, and normal brewing fuel.
&e2. &7Put a helmet in an enchanting table's item slot.
&e3. &7Put Phantom Repellant in its lapis slot.
&e4. &7Select the first offer to add Phantom Menace I.
&7The enchantment costs one level and one repellant.
&7No bookshelves are needed. Other enchantments stay.

&6Brewing recipe
&f water bottle + phantom membrane = Phantom Repellant
&7Use plain water bottles; other potions do not work.
&7Up to three bottles brew at once, as usual.
&7Repellant looks like a glowing awkward potion,
&7but is lapis underneath and cannot be consumed.

&6Supported helmets
&f leather, chainmail, copper, iron, golden,
&f diamond, netherite, and turtle helmets
&7Use a helmet without Phantom Menace already on it.
&7Books, pumpkins, heads, and other armor do not work.
&7The power only works while the helmet is worn.

&6After enchanting
&7A successful non-creative enchant returns one
&7empty glass bottle above the enchanting table.
&7Creative enchanting is free and returns no bottle.
&7Cancelled or unsuccessful enchants return none.
&7Repellant cannot be used as crafting lapis.

&6Limits
&7Only natural phantom pre-spawns for the wearer stop.
&7Other players, existing phantoms, and attacks are
&7unaffected. Command and spawner spawns still work.
&7This does not reset the player's insomnia timer.
&7Other plugins can cancel brewing or enchanting.
&7Keep this plugin installed for enchanted helmets;
&7removing its registry entry can discard the enchant.

&6Commands & permissions
&7There are no plugin commands or permissions.
&7Read this guide with &f/help phantommenace&7.

&6Server owners
&7Requires Paper 26.3 and Java 25. Restart the server
&7after install; custom enchantments need a full restart.
&7No config file, client mod, or resource pack is needed.
