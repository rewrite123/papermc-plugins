&6&lFlipDisc&r &7- flip eligible music discs to their B-side
&7One fixed pair per disc; flip back anytime. No random songs.

&6How to flip
&e1. &7Open your 2x2 inventory grid or a crafting table.
&e2. &7Put exactly one eligible disc in any slot, with no
&7other ingredients. Take the opposite disc from the result.
&e3. &7Craft it again to flip back, then play it in a jukebox.
&7Recipes are added to the recipe book when you join.
&7Normal crafting handles shift-click and inventory space.

&6Permanent pairs
&fCat &7<-> &fTears
&fBlocks &7<-> &f11
&fChirp &7<-> &fBounce
&f13 &7<-> &fMellohi
&fFar &7<-> &fStrad
&fMall &7<-> &fStal
&fWait &7<-> &fWard
&7Each pair is fixed and reversible; no cross-pair cycling.

&6Not flip-enabled
&7Pigstep, Otherside, 5, Relic, Creator, Creator (Music Box),
&7Precipice and Lava Chicken have no recipe.
&7Discs with an overridden jukebox playback component are
&7left unchanged so custom songs are not overwritten.
&7New discs in later Minecraft versions are not auto-paired.

&6Item data and automation
&7Names, lore and custom item data carry across the flip.
&7The output remains a vanilla disc for the selected song.
&7Crafter blocks cannot use these manual flip recipes.
&7Removing the plugin does not change existing discs.

&6Commands and permissions
&7There are no plugin commands, permissions or settings.
&7Open this guide with /help flipdisc.
