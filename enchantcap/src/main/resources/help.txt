&6&lEnchantCap&r &7- keep valid anvil work under 40 levels
&7Removes the anvil cutoff and charges at most &f39 levels&7.
&7Valid results, ingredients, conflicts and prior-work rules stay vanilla.

&6How to use it
&e1. &7Use an anvil as usual: repair, rename, or combine items/books.
&e2. &7The preview appears even when vanilla would say "Too Expensive".
&e3. &7You still need the displayed levels and any normal ingredients.
&7The limit applies to all players and normal anvil operations.

&6Costs and repair materials
&7All valid operations cap at &f39 levels&7; positive costs stay positive.
&7Material repairs scale by consumed materials / item crafting count.
&fPickaxe/axe 3 | sword/hoe 2 | shovel 1
&fHelmet 5 | chestplate 8 | leggings 7 | boots 4
&7Fractions round up. Other repairs and renames use capped vanilla cost.

&6Enchantments
&7Vanilla enchantment maximums apply unless an admin opts in below.
&7Compatible merges and conflicts still follow vanilla rules.
&7No enchanting-table, loot, trade, or existing-item changes.

&6Commands
&7Anvil use requires no permission.

&6Permission
&7&fenchantcap.admin &7(default: operators) manages the setting.
&7Other players can use anvils normally; no permission is required.

[admin:enchantcap.admin]
&6Admin command
&f/enchantcap max-additional-enchantment-levels &7- show limit
&f/enchantcap max-additional-enchantment-levels <0-254> &7- set limit

&6Admin setting
&fmax-additional-enchantment-levels: &e0&7 (default)
&70 keeps vanilla maximums; &f1&7 allows max + 1, and so on.
&7Range: &f0-254&7; resulting levels cannot exceed &f255&7.
&7Examples: Unbreaking IV or Efficiency VI with value &f1&7.
&7The command saves and applies immediately; manual edits need restart.
&7Changing the value closes open anvils so players can reopen previews.
&7Existing higher levels remain, but cannot be raised past the new limit.
[/admin]

&6Limits and tips
&7Other anvil plugins may replace costs or results after EnchantCap.
&7Restart after installing/removing plugins; do not hot-reload.
&7Use &f/help enchantcap &7for this guide and clickable project links.
