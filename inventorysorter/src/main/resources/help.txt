&6&lInventorySorter&r &7- combine and sort your storage
&7Sort items by category and material, combining
&7matching partial stacks first. Items never cross
&7between your inventory and a container.

&6How to sort
&e1. &7Empty your cursor and find an empty slot.
&e2. &7Hold Shift and click that same slot twice
&7within half a second (left or right click).
&e3. &7Keep the same inventory screen open; sorting
&7runs on the next server tick.
&7Use &f/sort&7 if you cannot find an empty slot.

&6What gets sorted
&7Your inventory: the 27 main storage slots only.
&7Hotbar, armor, offhand and crafting slots stay put.
&7Chest, double chest, barrel, placed shulker box
&7and your ender chest: the whole container.
&7Other screens: double-click your own storage slots.
&7The screen's special slots are never sorted.

&6Commands
&f/sort &7- sort container, or your storage otherwise
&f/sort player &7- sort your 27 storage slots
&f/sort container &7- sort an open supported container
&7Commands require an empty cursor. Container mode
&7needs a chest, barrel, shulker or ender chest open.

&6Ordering and safety
&7Groups and item material names sort alphabetically.
&7Matching stacks combine up to their stack limit;
&7custom names and other different metadata stay apart.
&7Empty slots move to the end; item counts and metadata
&7are preserved. Spectators cannot sort.
&7Permission: &finventorysorter.use &7(default: everyone).
&7Cancelled clicks, closing the screen or disconnecting
&7reset the double-click. Changes are rechecked before
&7the delayed sort is applied.
&7Type /help inventorysorter for this guide.
