&6&lBetterLanterns&r &7- better lanterns, soul candles and mob wards

&6What it does
&7Craft lanterns with candles, add invisible light around lanterns,
&7and block nearby natural hostile spawns with clear line of sight.
&7Craft soul candles for blue flames and small nearby light.

&6Recipes
&7Lantern (makes 1):
&f top: gold nugget . candle . gold nugget
&f mid: iron nugget . glass pane . iron nugget
&f bot: iron nugget iron nugget iron nugget
&7Soul candle (makes 4):
&f top: . candle .
&f mid: candle candle candle
&f bot: . soul sand .
&7Soul lantern (makes 1): use the lantern recipe with a soul
&7candle. Vanilla lantern and soul lantern recipes are replaced.
&7Recipes are visible in the recipe book.

&6How to use
&e1. &7Craft and place either lantern for the light halo and ward.
&e2. &7Craft soul candles; place on a solid surface, not a cake.
&e3. &7Light with flint and steel or a fire charge. Right-click
&7with an empty hand (without sneaking) to extinguish.

&6Light spread
&7Lanterns add invisible level-15 light through air up to 3
&7orthogonal block steps. Walls block paths; light bends around
&7corners. Occupied blocks and player lights are not overwritten.
&7Soul lanterns keep their own vanilla level-10 light; halo is the
&7same. Soul candles add light levels 2, 5, 8 or 11 to
&7adjacent air for 1, 2, 3 or 4 candles. Light stays white.

&6Spawn blocking
&7Lanterns block natural hostile mob spawns within &f24 blocks&7
&7in a sphere, only with direct line of sight. Solid shapes,
&7including glass, block sight. This does not affect other spawn
&7reasons, animals, existing mobs, or soul candles.
&7Only loaded and discovered lanterns count; no chunk is forced.

&6Commands, permissions and settings
&7This plugin has no commands, permissions, or config.yml.
&f/help betterlanterns &7- show this guide and GitHub links

&6Limits and maintenance
&7Existing loaded chunks are scanned incrementally (one chunk per
&7tick); recalculation handles up to 8 sources per tick.
&7Invisible lights and candle states persist in chunk data.
&7Disabling restores lights in loaded chunks; unloaded chunks are
&7cleaned when loaded again with this plugin. Before uninstalling,
&7load affected chunks while enabled and disable cleanly.
&7Soul candles cannot mix with normal candles or sit on cakes.
