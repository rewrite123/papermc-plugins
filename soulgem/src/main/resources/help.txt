&6&lSoulGem&r &7- charge gems with XP and enchant at a table
&7Replace lapis fuel with charged Soul Gems or Echo Soul Gems.
&7No player commands; use the recipes and table steps below.
&7Permission &fsoulgem.use &7defaults on.

&6Crafting recipes (shapeless)
&f1 lapis lazuli + 1 amethyst shard = 1 Soul Gem
&f1 lapis lazuli + 1 echo shard = 1 Echo Soul Gem
&7Gems cannot be used as crafting ingredients.

&6Charge a gem
&e1. &7Hold one uncharged gem in your off-hand.
&e2. &7Earn XP; the gem absorbs new XP toward one full level.
&e3. &7Use the charged, glowing gem in the table's lapis slot.
&7The charge target locks when it first absorbs XP; your bar XP is kept.
&7Partial charge persists. Only the off-hand gem charges; no stacks.
&7Excess XP from the last award goes to you. Both gem kinds cost equal XP.

&6Enchanting table
&7Put gear or a book in the item slot and charged gems in the fuel slot.
&7Pick an offer normally. Offer costs use vanilla levels and gem counts:
&fOffer 1: 1 gem, 1 level | Offer 2: 2 gems, 2 levels
&fOffer 3: 3 gems, 3 levels
&7Amethyst gems give normal rolls. Echo gems boost each roll by +1,
&7up to vanilla maximum +1. Preview shows the boosted level.
&7Enchanted gear can be upgraded; only improvements apply.
&7Conflicts and lower rolls are skipped. Empty/no-improvement offers cost none.
&7Plain lapis is refused. Creative players may enchant with an empty slot.

&6Permission and limits
&fsoulgem.use &7(default: everyone) allows charging and table enchanting.
&7Without it, you cannot use enchanting tables. Spectators and dead players
&7cannot use SoulGem. Enchanted books cannot be re-enchanted.
&7Treasure enchantments are not added to the vanilla offer pool.
&7Other plugins still see native prepare/enchant events.

&6Compatibility
&7With PhantomMenace enabled, its marked phantom repellant is left alone.
&7Old charged gems from 0.1.x convert when you join or open an inventory.
&7No resource pack, datapack, client mod, or configuration is required.
&7Use &f/help soulgem &7for this guide and clickable project links.
