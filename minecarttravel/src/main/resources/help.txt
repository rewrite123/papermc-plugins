&6&lMinecartTravel&r &7- boosted rides and lodestone fast travel

&6What it does
&7Earn a speed boost and ride registered lines between lodestones.
&7Both features are on by default; no items or fees are needed.
&7Type &f/help minecarttravel&7 for this guide and links.

&6How to ride
&e1. &f/minecarttravel fast&7 turns both features on.
&e2. &7Ride continuously above &f6 blocks/second&7.
&e3. &7After &f15 seconds&7, safe straight track boosts toward
&790 blocks/second. After &f30 seconds total&7, fast travel
&7starts on a registered line in the direction you are moving.
&7These are defaults; lag makes real time longer.
&7Stopping, derailing, or losing qualifying speed resets time.

&6Register a line
&e1. &7Build a line with a real lodestone at each end.
&7Each end must have flat, straight rail directly into it.
&e2. &f/minecarttravel register&7 while in a cart, or run it
&7before boarding (you have 60 seconds to get in).
&e3. &7Start within &f16 rails&7 of an end; ride to the other.
&7The action bar reports recorded rails and turns.
&7The line registers at the far lodestone. Switches and
&7branches are recorded as the cart actually travels them.
&7You must keep riding; dismounting cancels registration.
&f lodestone | rail = rail = ... = rail | lodestone

&6Your options
&f/minecarttravel boost [on|off] &7- toggle only the boost
&f/minecarttravel fasttravel [on|off] &7- toggle travel
&f/minecarttravel normal &7- turn both features off
&f/minecarttravel fast &7- turn both features on
&7Opt-outs persist. Fast travel can work with boost off.
&7Only a single-player rideable cart teleports; cargo carts
&7and carts with other passengers do not fast travel.

&6Commands
&f/minecarttravel [status] &7- choices, ride time, route status
&f/minecarttravel register [cancel] &7- register or stop a line
&f/minecarttravel settings &7- show timing and speed values
&7Alias: &f/minecartfasttravel&7. Settings are public.

&6Safety and limits
&7Boosts need straight, level, supported and loaded track
&7ahead, with passable headroom and no nearby entities.
&7Curves, slopes, brakes, obstructions, gaps and unloaded
&7track return the cart to normal speed. No chunks load for
&7boosting. Normal powered-rail brakes are not overridden.
&7A line is removed if rails change shape or either lodestone
&7is removed. Re-register after fixing it.
&7Registration limit: &f65,536 rails, 32 turns or slope
&7changes. Line edits are rechecked when chunks load.
&7Owners can use /minecarttravel rescan after offline edits.

&7save-interval-ticks 600. Work budgets are global.
