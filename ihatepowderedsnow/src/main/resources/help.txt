&6&lIHatePowderedSnow&r &7- replace generated powder snow with snow blocks

&6What it does
&7During new chunk generation, every powder snow block becomes a
&7solid snow block. The terrain shape is kept; snow traps are gone.

&6How to use
&e1. &7Install on Paper and restart before exploring new terrain.
&e2. &7Explore or generate chunks after the plugin is enabled.
&7No player interaction or setup is needed.

&6Mechanics and scope
&7Runs after vanilla terrain/features in a world-generation
&7populator. It checks all 256 columns from min to max build height.
&7Only POWDER_SNOW becomes SNOW_BLOCK; thin snow and other blocks
&7are unchanged. New worlds are covered too.
&7Already-generated chunks are not scanned on load. Player-placed
&7powder snow, buckets and cauldrons are unchanged.
&7Generation plugins that add powder snow after this populator may
&7still place it. The plugin doesn't replace the world generator.

&6Commands, permissions and settings
&7This plugin has no commands, permissions, or config.yml.
&f/help ihatepowderedsnow &7- show this guide and GitHub links

&6Tips and limits
&7A standard -64 to 320 chunk requires 98,304 block checks.
&7There is no recurring scan or chunk-load processing.
&7Converted blocks remain ordinary snow blocks if you uninstall.
&7Back up worlds before adding world-generation changes.
